English events in Warsaw, all in one place

Looking for things to do in Warsaw in English? Events are scattered across Luma, Meetup, Eventbrite and Resident Advisor, and each site only shows its own. wyd warsaw? pulls them together every hour, so you can see what's on today, this weekend or next month: meetups, stand-up comedy, language exchanges, tech talks, startup and networking nights, art, wellness, sport and club nights.

What's on in Warsaw this weekend in English?

Tap a Saturday or Sunday in the calendar to see everything that day, or use Surprise me and choose "This weekend" for a random pick within your budget.

Where do the events come from?

Luma, Meetup, Eventbrite and Resident Advisor. Events posted on more than one site show up once, with every link.

How do you know an event is in English?

It reads the event's title and description and checks which language they're written in. Bilingual events count. Club nights are listed too, since the music speaks for itself. Switch to "Everything" to see Polish and other events as well.

Are there free events in Warsaw?

Plenty. Look for the Free tag, or set Surprise me's budget to Free.

Put it in your calendar

  1. Tap Add to Google Calendar and confirm. It shows up as its own calendar you can toggle.
  2. Apple Calendar: File, then New Calendar Subscription, and paste the feed link.
  3. New events appear on their own. Google refreshes subscribed calendars every few hours.
